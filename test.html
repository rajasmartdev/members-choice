About us

Who we
are.

Members Choice is an Australian AI insurance platform built for associations and their members. We enable associations to offer their members a smarter way to obtain insurance — making it effortless for members to access multiple insurance products on one platform, and easy for associations to partner and be rewarded.

Our purpose

Why we built
Members Choice.

We built Members Choice on a simple belief: members deserve a better way to obtain insurance, and associations deserve to be rewarded for serving them.

So we created a platform that does both — using smart technology and trusted insurers to deliver real value to members, with less effort and lasting rewards for the associations behind them.
