Our Promise

Clear benefits. No compromises.

Members Choice is built to put members first — with honest advice,
real people, and support you can rely on.

What you get

What's included in every quote.

  • Personalised quotes across multiple products — not a generic template.
  • Honest comparisons against what you already have.
  • Gaps in your current cover flagged clearly.
  • A real person to talk to before, during, or after — same number every time.

What we don't do

A few things we won't do.

  • Spam you. One quote. One follow-up if you want it. That's it.
  • Sell your details. Your information stays with Members Choice.
  • Push cover you don't need. We quote what suits your situation.
  • Replace a real person when you need one.
