Frequently Asked Questions.

About Members Choice

Learn more about who we are and how we work.

Who is Members Choice?
Members Choice is an Australian insurance platform built exclusively for association members, offering tailored cover at competitive rates.
How are you different from other insurers?
We work exclusively with associations to provide member-only benefits, personalised quotes, and an onshore Australian claims team — not a call centre overseas.

About Cover

Information about products and your cover.

What products do you offer?
We offer eight products: Health, Car, Home & Contents, Life, Income Protection, Travel, Pet Insurance, and Critical Illness Cover.
Are there waiting periods?
Waiting periods vary by product. Health insurance typically has waiting periods for extras and some hospital treatments. Your policy documents will outline specific periods.
What if I have a pre-existing condition?
Pre-existing conditions are assessed individually. Get a quote and our team will walk you through what's covered and any applicable conditions.

About Quotes

Questions about getting and using our quotes.

How do I get a free quote?
Click 'Get My Free Quote' on any page and answer a few simple questions. We'll return your personalised quote instantly — no obligation required.
How long does the quote take?
Most quotes take under two minutes to complete. For more complex products like life insurance, it may take a little longer.
Is the quote actually free?
Yes, completely free. There's no obligation to proceed and no credit card required at the quote stage.
Will you spam me afterwards?
No. We'll send your quote and follow up once. You can unsubscribe at any time and we'll never sell your data to third parties.

For Associations

How we partner with associations.

How do associations partner with you?
Associations sign a simple partnership agreement and we handle everything — setup, marketing materials, member communications, and reporting.
Can our association get a co-branded landing page?
Yes. Every partner association gets a dedicated co-branded landing page with your logo, colours, and member-specific messaging at no extra cost.

Not happy with a decision?.

You have the right to a review. Start with our internal disputes team and we'll examine it fresh, with a response within 30 days. If you remain unsatisfied, you can escalate at no cost to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), the independent industry ombudsman.
