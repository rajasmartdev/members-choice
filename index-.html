Three ways to lodge.

Online.

Sign in to your account, upload your documents, done. Available 24/7.

By phone.

Call 1300 XXX XXX, Mon–Fri 8am–6pm AEST. For urgent after-hours claims, our 24/7 line is 1300 XXX XXX.

In writing.

Email claims@memberschoice.com.au.

What you'll need.

Your policy number.

A completed claim form, online or downloadable.

Supporting evidence — medical reports, invoices, police reports, receipts.

Bank details for payment.
